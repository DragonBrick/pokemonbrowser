P
Pokémon Browser
Browse all Pokémon with sortable columns, stat totals, and type badges. Double-click a row to open the detail panel showing stats, evolution chains (with sprites and clickable nodes), and a full move list. Use the search bar to find Pokémon by name, and add filters for generation, type, stat range, total range, moves, and evolution stage. Resize columns by dragging their right edge.
M
Moves
Search and filter moves by name, type, category, min learners, priority, or which Pokémon learn them. Columns show power, accuracy, PP, priority, damage class, and description. Double-click a move chip in any filter to jump to its detail view, where you can see all Pokémon that learn it and filter that list.
A
Abilities
Browse abilities with their full descriptions. Filter by generation or by which Pokémon have them. Double-click any ability chip to navigate to its detail view. Each ability entry shows the description and a list of Pokémon that can have it.
T
Team Builder
Build competitive teams across 10 save slots. Each slot supports a Pokémon, ability, item, nature, Tera type, level, custom EV/IV spreads, and four moves. Use Import/Export to share teams in Pokémon Showdown format. Double-click the team name to rename it. The 3-second auto-save keeps your progress safe.
W
Wallpaper Generator
Generate a custom 1920×1080 wallpaper for any Pokémon. Search for a Pokémon, click Generate, and the app draws a canvas with the official artwork, type-colored accents, stat bars, and a Pokédex fact. Download the result as a high-quality PNG image.
S
Speedrun
Test your navigation speed: a random start entity and target entity are chosen. Navigate through the app to reach the target in as few clicks as possible. Customize which entity types (Pokémon, Move, Ability) are used for start and target, or pick specific entities. History is saved between sessions.
G
Games
Play Who's That Pokémon (guess the Pokémon from its silhouette) or Pokedle (deduce the Pokémon by guessing and checking type, generation, height, and weight feedback). Both support daily and endless modes. Use the Checklist tab to track your progress through the mainline games. Scores persist during your session.
E
Tools
Three tools in one tab. Type Calculator: pick a move type and one or two target types to see the effectiveness multiplier. Comparer: compare up to 5 Pokémon, Moves, or Items side-by-side — Pokémon comparisons can switch between Table and Hexagon views, and double-click any row to open its detail. Links: browse useful sites and keep your own saved links.
C
Collection
Search for Pokémon TCG cards using the PokéTCG API and build your personal collection with PSA grading and estimated values. Browse the Main view for random cards from recent sets, or switch to Collection view to see your owned cards. Cards are saved to your browser's local storage.
I
Items
Browse all held items and key items from the Pokémon games. Search by name, filter by category (Berry, Choice, Type Boost, Evolution, Battle, Mega Stone, Misc), and double-click any item to see its full category listing. Each item shows a short description of its effect.
